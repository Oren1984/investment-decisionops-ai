title: Collaboration and sharing
category: Collaboration
summary: Inviting brokers, sharing safely and revoking access.
routes: /collaboration
---
1. **Invite** a broker or trader: the app creates a one-time link (no email is sent; you send it). They set a password and get the *collaborator* role.
2. **Share** a decision, document, note or meeting with *view* or *view and comment* permission, optionally with an expiry.
3. Collaborators see only what you shared — never portfolio totals, orders, broker data or settings. Raw quote data is withheld from shared decisions; portfolio context is excluded unless you opt in.
4. **Revoke** a share or a person: access ends immediately, including open live-update streams.

Sharing analysis with others can approach regulated activity in some jurisdictions. See [Governance](/help/governance).
