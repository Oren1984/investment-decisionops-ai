title: Reviews: process versus outcome
category: Decisions
summary: How to review a decision and why the two grades are separate.

---
A good result does not prove good reasoning, and a bad result does not prove bad reasoning.

1. Compare each **assumption** (A1, A2…) with what happened and mark whether it held.
2. Grade **process quality** with the rubric (evidence, alternatives, sizing, plan, timing; 0–2 each).
3. Record the **outcome** (return and benchmark) separately.
4. Capture **lessons** with tags; search them on the Decisions page.

The *Process vs. outcome* grid on the Decisions page shows patterns across decisions.
