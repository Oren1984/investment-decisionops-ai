- **Owner control:** only you approve, submit, share, change limits, erase data or (in future) activate Live.
- **Agents:** read/compute-only tools; proposals are inert.
- **Versions:** prompts are versioned files; the model name and digest are recorded per step.
- **Incident runbook:** stop → assess → reconcile → document → lessons.

This is a personal workspace. It does not claim compliance with any regulation. Before any use beyond strictly personal use, get qualified advice on investment-advice rules (for example Israel's Investment Advice Law), privacy obligations for collaborator data, and market-data redistribution terms.
