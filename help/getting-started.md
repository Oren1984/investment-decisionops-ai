Investment DecisionOps AI turns an investment question into a **traceable decision record**: sources, data timestamps, assumptions, counterarguments, scenarios, checks, your rationale, any orders and a later review.

## A five-minute tour

1. **Overview** shows your portfolio (synthetic in Mock mode), watchlist, a chart, agent activity, decisions and anything that needs attention.
2. **Markets** shows the clock and session state of any venue you pick, with holidays, lunch breaks and countdowns.
3. **Decisions → New question** starts a research run. Watch the workflow bar move through the stages.
4. Open the decision: read the **evidence package**, then record **accept / reject / defer** with your rationale.
5. Optionally create an **order ticket**, preview it, approve it and submit it to the Mock broker.
6. Later, **review** the decision: grade the process and the outcome separately.

Everything in Mock mode is simulated. See [Modes](/help/modes).
