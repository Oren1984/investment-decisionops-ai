---
title: Alert: order uncertain for more than 2 minutes
category: Alerts
summary: A submission outcome is still unknown.
routes: /orders
---
The broker did not confirm a submission. **Do not resubmit manually.**

1. Open Orders → the order, and press **Reconcile now**.
2. If the broker is disconnected, restore the connection first.
3. After the grace period the order becomes *Not found* (safe to prepare a resubmission) or *Acknowledged*.
