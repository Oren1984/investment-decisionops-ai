# NVIDIA sample research note

Synthetic sample note for the offline demo. All figures are fictional and exist only to exercise the Knowledge Library.

## Thesis

The synthetic thesis is that data-center accelerator demand keeps growing through 2027. In this fictional scenario, data-center revenue grew 38% year over year in the latest quarter, while gaming revenue was flat.

## Valuation

The stock trades at 31 times forward earnings in this sample, above its five-year average of 27 times. A premium valuation leaves little room for disappointment if growth slows.

## Risks

Key risks include export restrictions on advanced chips, customer concentration among four hyperscale buyers, and competition from custom accelerators designed in-house by cloud providers. A slowdown in capital spending by the largest buyers would hit revenue quickly.

## Position sizing

Because volatility is high, the note suggests keeping any single semiconductor position below 8% of a diversified portfolio.
