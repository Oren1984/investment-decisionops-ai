# Japanese automakers (sample)

Synthetic sample note for the offline demo. Fictional figures.

## Toyota

Toyota Motor is the largest automaker by unit sales in this sample. Hybrid vehicles made up 41% of its global sales. A weaker yen boosts reported profits because a large share of revenue is earned abroad.

## Trading hours

The Tokyo Stock Exchange trades from 09:00 to 15:30 Japan time with a lunch break from 11:30 to 12:30.

## Risks

Tariffs on vehicle imports into the United States are the main policy risk. A sharp yen appreciation would reduce reported earnings.
