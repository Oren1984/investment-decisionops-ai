# USD/ILS outlook (sample)

Synthetic sample note for the offline demo. Fictional figures.

## Drivers

The shekel has been supported by strong technology-sector inflows and a current-account surplus of about 4% of GDP in this sample. The Bank of Israel policy rate is assumed at 4.0% in this fictional note.

## Hedging

For an investor whose home currency is ILS, unhedged US equity holdings carry currency risk. A 5% appreciation of the shekel would reduce the ILS value of USD assets by roughly 5% before any change in the underlying prices.

## Risks

Geopolitical events can trigger sharp, short-lived shekel depreciation. Hedging costs depend on the interest-rate differential between the US and Israel.
