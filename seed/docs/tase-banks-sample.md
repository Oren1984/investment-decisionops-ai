# Israeli banks on TASE (sample)

Synthetic sample note for the offline demo. Fictional figures.

## Trading week change

The Tel Aviv Stock Exchange moved to a Monday-to-Friday trading week on 5 January 2026. Friday sessions close early, at 13:50 Israel time.

## Sector view

Bank Leumi and Bank Hapoalim are the two largest listed banks. In this sample, their return on equity was about 15% last year, helped by higher interest margins. Dividend payout ratios were raised to 40%.

## Access

Execution on TASE is outside the first broker's verified scope in this workspace. Holdings at an Israeli bank can be recorded as manual holdings.

## Risks

Net interest margins would compress if the Bank of Israel cuts rates. Credit losses could rise in a slowdown, especially in construction lending.
