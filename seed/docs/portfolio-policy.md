# Personal investment policy (sample)

Synthetic sample policy for the offline demo.

## Allocation targets

Target allocation: 60% global equities, 25% bonds and cash, 15% Israeli equities. Rebalance when any sleeve drifts more than 5 percentage points from target.

## Rules

No single stock above 10% of the portfolio. No leverage and no short selling. Every new position needs a written thesis, at least one counterargument, and a review date.

## Review cadence

Each decision is reviewed after 30 days and again after 6 months. Process quality is graded separately from the investment result.
